Choosing the method

OptionMethod
(none) or --gfn 2GFN2-xTB, the default
--gfn 1GFN1-xTB
--gfn 0GFN0-xTB, no SCF
--gfnffGFN-FF force field

Use GFN2 unless there is a specific reason not to. The differences are summarised in the table in chapter 16 of the ORCA guide.

Charge and spin

xtb takes the number of unpaired electrons instead of the multiplicity: --uhf 2 for a triplet, --uhf 1 for a doublet. ORCA's multiplicity 3 is xtb's --uhf 2, which is easy to get wrong when copying settings across. 

xtb anion.xyz   --chrg -1
xtb triplet.xyz --uhf 2
xtb cation.xyz  --chrg 1 --uhf 1

Files named .CHRG and .UHF in the working directory, each holding one number, are read without any options. CREST reads them too, so for ions and radicals it pays to create them once.

Geometry optimisation

xtb ala.xyz --opt           # default convergence (normal)
xtb ala.xyz --opt tight     # use tight or better before frequencies

From loosest to tightest the levels are crude, sloppy, loose, lax, normal, tight, vtight and extreme.

Check that the optimisation finished

The output must contain GEOMETRY OPTIMIZATION CONVERGED. xtbopt.xyz is written even when the run stops at the iteration limit, so its existence alone does not mean convergence.

Frequencies and thermochemistry

xtb xtbopt.xyz --hess        # Hessian at the given geometry
xtb ala.xyz --ohess tight    # optimise, then Hessian

A Hessian run goes on to zero-point energy, enthalpy and entropy and prints TOTAL FREE ENERGY. Low-frequency modes are interpolated towards free rotors instead of harmonic oscillators by default (mRRHO), which keeps their entropy from being inflated. If the structure is not a minimum, imaginary frequencies are printed as negative numbers.

Example: gas-phase optimisation and frequencies

Alanine dipeptide optimised in the gas phase, followed by frequencies.

xtb ala.xyz --gfn 2 --opt tight   > opt.out
xtb ala.xyz --gfn 2 --ohess tight > ohess.out
QuantityValue
Total energy (Eh)−32.975847892266
HOMO–LUMO gap (eV)4.480
φ / ψ (°)−81.4 / 74.0 (C7eq)
Free energy (Eh, 298 K)−32.833577987317
Lowest frequencies (cm⁻¹)51.78, 63.82, 81.38, 102.62 (no imaginary frequencies)
Wall time0.52 s for the optimisation, 0.68 s for optimisation plus frequencies (8 cores)

The optimisation ended in C7eq, where an intramolecular N–H···O=C hydrogen bond closes a seven-membered ring. There are no imaginary frequencies, so it is a true minimum. The zero-point energy is 0.1806 Eh (113 kcal/mol); the free energy adds thermal corrections and entropy on top. There are three modes below 100 cm⁻¹, and it is the entropy of low-frequency modes like these that the mRRHO correction changes most.

Constraints and scans

To fix or scan a distance or dihedral, pass a detailed input file with --input. Atoms are numbered from 1 in xyz-file order (ORCA counts from 0). 

$constrain
   force constant=1.0
   dihedral: 2, 4, 5, 7, -60.0
$end
$scan
   1: -180.0, 180.0, 37
$end
xtb ala.xyz --opt --input scan.inp > scan.out

The 1: in $scan refers to the first constraint defined in $constrain. This example steps φ from −180° to 180° in 37 points and relaxes everything else at each point; the results collect in xtbscan.log. To keep atoms from moving at all, list them in a $fix block, for example atoms: 1-5.
