About Me

Hello! I'm a final-year B.Sc. (Honours) Chemistry student at the National University of Singapore, working in computational chemistry under A/P Liviu Ungur.

Across three consecutive undergraduate research projects (UROPS) I have progressed from multireference electronic-structure treatment of lanthanide single-molecule magnets, to classical molecular dynamics at metal–solvent interfaces, and currently to the development of a reactive machine-learning force field for iron oxidation. The common thread is using computational methods to connect electronic structure to macroscopic behaviour.

My academic interests span inorganic chemistry, magnetochemistry & molecular spintronics, and interfacial / computational studies of materials. Alongside research I have been actively tutoring chemistry, physics, biology, and mathematics at both AP and general-curriculum levels since high school.
