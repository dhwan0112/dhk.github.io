Donghwan KIM

Final-year chemistry student at the National University of Singapore, working on computational chemistry in Asst. Prof. Liviu Ungur’s group.

So far that has meant three projects: multireference calculations on a Dy3 single-molecule magnet, classical molecular dynamics of a benzene/ethanol mixture on copper, and, for my final-year project, a neural network potential for the surface oxidation of iron. I am most interested in inorganic chemistry, molecular magnetism and spintronics, and simulations of interfaces.

I also write guides, in Korean and English, to the programs I use, and I have tutored chemistry, physics, biology and maths one-on-one since 2020.
