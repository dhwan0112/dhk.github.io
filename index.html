Donghwan KIM

Final-year chemistry student at the National University of Singapore, working on computational chemistry in A/P Liviu Ungur’s group.

So far that has meant three projects: multireference calculations on a Dy3 single-molecule magnet, classical molecular dynamics of a benzene/ethanol mixture on copper, and, for my final-year project, a machine-learning potential for the oxidation of iron. I am most interested in inorganic chemistry, molecular magnetism and spintronics, and simulations of interfaces.

I also write guides, in Korean and English, to the programs I use, and I have tutored chemistry, physics, biology and maths one-on-one since 2020.

Research

  1. 2026–

    A reactive machine-learning potential for iron oxidation

    Final-year project (CM4288). The training data come from periodic DFT in Quantum ESPRESSO.

  2. 2025

    Benzene and ethanol on Cu(100) and Cu(111)

    UROPS (CM3288, CM3289). Classical MD in LAMMPS comparing OPLS-AA with TraPPE-UA, and PPPM with MSM electrostatics. Poster at the NUS Science Summer Institute 2025 symposium.

  3. 2024

    Exchange and anisotropy in a Dy3 triangle

    UROPS (CM2288). CASSCF/NEVPT2 with spin–orbit coupling in ORCA; exchange coupling fitted to magnetic data with POLY_ANISO.
